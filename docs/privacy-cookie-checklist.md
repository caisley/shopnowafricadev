# Privacy and cookie configuration checklist

The first release intentionally contains no analytics, advertising, chat widget, embedded tracking, or marketing script. Essential session, CSRF, and saved-preference behaviour remains necessary for forms and security.

Before enabling any optional service, document its vendor, purpose, cookie names, data recipients, retention, international transfer, lawful basis or consent route, and opt-out/withdrawal behaviour. Add the vendor to the Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy, load it only after the appropriate consent state, and retest that Reject optional leaves no optional script active.

Confirm the official controller identity and privacy contact, the request handling response process, identity verification approach, retention schedule for form submissions and uploads, backup retention, processor agreements, and the legal review status under the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and any other law that applies to SNA’s actual operations.
