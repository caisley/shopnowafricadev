# Pre-launch checklist

## SNA-owned placeholders

- Replace the official legal/business identity, email, telephone, physical address, privacy contact, and social links.
- Review the slogan, establishment year, service descriptions, and Ghana/Africa positioning.
- Add only authorised client names, testimonials, awards, certifications, partnerships, project images, case studies, and verified outcomes.
- Replace the placeholder notification and From email values; test delivery and response ownership.
- Decide whether protected storage stays outside `public_html` or is hosted in the included protected directory.

## Hosting and operations

- Confirm PHP 8+, `fileinfo`, HTTPS, cPanel permissions, and backup coverage.
- Configure HTTP-to-HTTPS redirect and verify session cookies on the HTTPS site.
- Test `mail()` or the approved SMTP transport with a real mailbox.
- Submit and review contact, RFP, RFQ, upload, and privacy requests.
- Confirm the response team, submission retention period, backup access, and deletion process.
- Recheck sitemap, robots, canonical domain, 404 status, and Open Graph previews after the domain is live.

## Legal and accessibility review

- Have the Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy, Terms, Disclaimer, data rights wording, and retention assumptions reviewed by qualified counsel.
- Confirm the actual privacy controller/processor arrangements and any international transfers.
- Test keyboard navigation, visible focus, form errors, mobile menu, reduced motion, contrast, and screen-reader landmarks on the production domain.
